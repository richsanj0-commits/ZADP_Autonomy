3 · Safe boundary (Minkowski erosion)

Each edge moves inward by a margin, as in Step 1 of the method. The truck's GPS centre stays inside the green zone, so the whole truck body stays inside the dump area. The largest margins in the fleet are used, so every truck is safe.

Boustrophedon partition

A sweep line crosses the safe zone. At every critical point (a corner where the shape's width changes) it cuts a new cell, so each cell is a simple strip without notches. Cells deeper than one bay are cut again into bays. A bay = one branch road with piles on both sides, sized from the trucks: one truck works one bay half at a time.

Entry, spine and branches

The spine is the main two-way road (IN + OUT) from the entry along the sweep direction to the back. Each bay half gets a temporary one-truck branch road off the spine. Branches and spine are filled last, deepest part first — like water rising.

Best entry runs the full dispatch from the current entry and the 4 longest edges and keeps the best run with no collision or deadlock (takes 1–5 min on big sites; in tests up to +7 points).

Step 3 · Hexagonal pile grid

One grid per truck type. Rows are (2r + spacing)·√3/2 apart and every other row is shifted half a step. Pile centre must be inside a zone; the pile circle must be inside the dump area and must not cover the lane. Spacing 0 = touching; negative = overlap.

Human operators place piles closer than their size, so neighbouring piles merge. Overlap packs more: in tests 0 m ≈ 77 %, −1 m ≈ 87 %, −2 m ≈ 94 % of the dump area.

Lane (2 one-way lanes)

The lane runs from the entry to the back. It is filled last, deepest part first — like water rising: a part of the lane gets piles only after every spot deeper than it is full.

Dispatch method

Baseline: today's practice, fixed spot points with 7.38 m between piles. Grid + remove: the smallest truck's hex grid; a bigger pile cancels the grid spots it covers. Touching: each pile goes as deep as possible, touching piles already there.
All three use the same checks: spot free · zone not locked (Rule 2) · truck can drive there and back (one-way lanes, Rule 1) · lane filled last, deepest first.
